A court draws a line around Tabu’s face, name and voice

150+URLs ordered
down36 HRSto comply

Justice Jyoti Singh of the Delhi High Court has granted Tabu an ex parte interim injunction over her name, image and voice — naming AI, deepfakes and face morphing explicitly. Google must pull 34 URLs, Meta 106, X three, with Reddit and eBay named too. Some of it was AI-generated obscene content built from her own film scenes.

“Not confined to commercial loss — it can also affect privacy, personality and the right to live with dignity.”

The 14 August screen war has started early

₹35 CRSpider-Man,
second Sunday

PVR Inox Pictures, distributing Batwara 1947, is asking single screens for every show — and in multiplexes for seven shows a day in a two-screener, rising to fifteen in a five-screener. Awarapan 2’s side will share.

A two-screen cinema has about ten shows in a day. If one film takes seven, there is very little left — and Spider-Man: Brand New Day is still taking ₹35 crore on a second Sunday, so nobody wants to drop it either. Advance booking had not opened as of today.

In eleven days

Salman and Sanjay Dutt take 21 August

21 AUGIndia
release

7 Dogs, with Salman Khan and Sanjay Dutt, is set for an India release on 21 August — one week behind the 14 August pile-up, and into whatever screens that fight leaves standing.

That is the interesting part of the date, not the date itself. A week is long enough for the Independence Day releases to have shown their hand, and short enough to still catch the holiday tail.

Akshay Kumar to play Rajesh Khanna

Trade reporting has Akshay Kumar playing Rajesh Khanna in the R.D. Burman biopic being made by Farhan Akhtar and Neeraj Pandey. India’s first superstar, played by one of the ones who came after.

Carried as a scoop rather than an announcement, so treat it as reported and not confirmed — nobody involved has said it on the record yet.

Ajay Devgn is reported to have replaced Anup Soni as the host of Crime Patrol, with fifteen episodes already in the can.

Soni hosted it long enough that the show and the voice became the same thing to most people watching, which makes this a bigger change on screen than a hosting credit usually is.
