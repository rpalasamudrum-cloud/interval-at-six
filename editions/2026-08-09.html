Ramayana goes out to the world first — and comes home on Diwali

6 NOVWorld8 NOVIndia

Namit Malhotra has confirmed it. International territories get Ramayana on 6 November. India gets it two days later, on Diwali.

That order is backwards, and deliberately so. Almost every big Indian release opens at home and lets overseas follow — this one flips it, because if you are adapting the Ramayana, no date on the calendar beats the festival of lights.

₹400 crore: a Hollywood record just fell in India

₹462 CRIndia gross,
week one

Spider-Man: Brand New Day has crossed ₹400 crore here — past Avatar: The Way of Water — making it the biggest Hollywood release India has ever had. It opened on 30 July, a day before the rest of the world.

One caveat that gets made every time: that figure is week-one reporting, not a final total. Numbers move for weeks.
