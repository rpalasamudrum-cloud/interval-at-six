Panel one — the epic picks its day
01
Out to the world on 6 November. Home for Diwali on 8 November.

Producer Namit Malhotra has confirmed a split release for Ramayana: international territories on 6 November, India two days later, on Diwali.

It reverses the usual order. Ordinarily India goes first and the diaspora waits; here the overseas run opens the film and the home release is timed to land on the festival itself. For a film adapting the Ramayana, that is the whole argument for the date.

Reported Bollywood Hungama, 8 August 2026 — Ramayana’s release strategy revealed

Panel two — a record falls
02
400 CR

Spider-Man: Brand New Day has crossed ₹400 crore in India and become the highest-grossing Hollywood release the country has had, passing the lifetime net of Avatar: The Way of Water.

It opened here on 30 July, a day ahead of the global date — and by the close of week one the India gross was being reported at roughly ₹462 crore. Box-office numbers move; read that one as of the date on this edition.

Reported Deccan Herald — Highest Hollywood grosser in India

Panel three — five days to the comeback
03
14 August. Censor cleared. Two hours twenty.

Awarapan 2 has completed its censor formalities for a 14 August release. Emraan Hashmi returns to the role nineteen years on, with a locked runtime of two hours twenty and the trailer out since 6 August.

Trade sites are calling an opening north of ₹11 crore. That is a projection, not a result — the film has not released.

04

Sunny Deol, Karan Deol and Preity G Zinta will meet Partition survivors and visit the Partition Museum on the Punjab leg of the promotional tour for Batwara 1947.

Like life coming full circle.

Jugal Hansraj, the child at the centre of Shekhar Kapur’s Masoom in 1983, has joined Masoom: The Next Generation — forty-three years on from the film that introduced him.

His own words for it are in the balloon above, and they are the reason this one gets a panel rather than a line.

Desk notes

What is confirmed

The Ramayana dates, the Spider-Man record, the Awarapan 2 release date and runtime, the Batwara 1947 promotional plan, the Masoom casting, the Governor chart position and the TVF announcement are each carried by the outlet linked under their panel, on or before the date of this edition.

What is not a fact yet

  • The ₹11 crore opening for Awarapan 2. It is a trade projection for a film that releases on 14 August. It is marked as a projection on the panel and it will not be repeated as a result.
  • Box-office totals move. The ₹462 crore India gross for Spider-Man: Brand New Day is week-one reporting, not a closing figure.

The standing rule

No panel ships without a source link. Nothing goes in from memory, and nothing goes in because it would make a good panel. If a story cannot be sourced by the time the edition is drawn, it waits for tomorrow or it is dropped.
