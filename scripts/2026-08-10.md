> line was that misuse of this kind is not confined to commercial loss, and that
> it can also affect privacy, personality, and the right to live with dignity.
>
> That is the third or fourth such order in about two years, and the direction
> of travel is not subtle. If you make things on the internet about people who
> act for a living, this is the ground you are now standing on.
