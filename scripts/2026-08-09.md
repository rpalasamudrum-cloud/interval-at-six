**On screen:** swipe to card 1, hold full frame. Slow push in. At 0:40 punch in
on the stat block — **6 NOV / 8 NOV**.
**Lower third from 0:22:** Bollywood Hungama, 8 Aug 2026.

> Namit Malhotra has confirmed how *Ramayana* goes out. International territories
> get it on the sixth of November. India gets it two days later, on the eighth —
> which is Diwali.
>
> Read that order again, because it is backwards. Almost every big Indian release
> opens at home first and lets the overseas run follow. This one opens abroad and
> holds India back.
>
> There is a reason worth the two days. If you are releasing an adaptation of the
> Ramayana, opening it in India on any date other than the festival of lights is
> leaving something on the table that money cannot buy. So the diaspora gets the
> film first, and India gets the occasion.
>
> Whether the two-day gap survives contact with piracy is the interesting
> question, and nobody has answered it yet.
