How it works

Six slots

The lead, the money, what releases next, the quiet one, the human one, the small screen. Same six every day.

Ninety seconds

Full-screen cards you swipe or arrow through. Built for a phone first.

Five minutes

Every edition also goes out as a five-minute video, cut to the same six cards.

No photos

Nothing is lifted. No stills, no paparazzi shots, nobody’s likeness used without asking.

The promise. Nothing here is written from memory. Stories are pulled from the trades on the morning of the edition, and if one cannot be sourced by the time the cards are built, it waits or it is dropped. Forecasts are labelled forecasts. Corrections run at the top of the next edition.
